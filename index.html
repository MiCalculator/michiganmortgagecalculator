Glossary Tap to expand

Administrative fees: 1% admin fee is applicable in some taxing jurisdictions.

Estimates only: Loan terms, taxes, and fees vary by property and situation. Contact us for a more detailed, property-specific analysis.

Millage: Millage rates vary by county, local unit, and school district and are subject to change.

PRE / Non-PRE: “Primary residence” reflects PRE vs non-PRE. Rentals and second homes typically use non-PRE rates.

Special Assessments: Not included. Roads, water/sewer, sidewalks, and other assessments may apply and can significantly affect your total tax bill.

Taxable Value (TV): This calculator assumes taxable value is 50% of the sale price.

Uncapping: This estimate assumes taxable value resets to 50% of the sale price. First-year property taxes are based on the seller’s taxable value as of December 31 of the prior year. Contact us for a property-specific analysis.
