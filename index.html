Glossary Tap to expand
PRE (Principal Residence Exemption) Michigan’s exemption that reduces the school operating portion of property taxes for an owner-occupied primary residence. Non-PRE generally applies to rentals/second homes.
Millage Property tax rate per $1,000 of taxable value. Millage varies by local unit (city/township), county, school district, and other authorities.
Taxable Value (TV) The value used to calculate property taxes in Michigan. TV is generally capped under Proposal A and uncaps on transfer of ownership.
PMI Private Mortgage Insurance — often required when down payment is under 20% on conventional loans. Usually priced as an annual % and paid monthly.
Escrow Monthly amounts collected by the lender for taxes/insurance and paid when due.
Amortization A schedule showing how each payment is split between principal and interest over the loan term.
