Glossary Tap to expand
PRE (Principal Residence Exemption) Michigan’s exemption that reduces the school operating portion of property taxes for an owner-occupied primary residence. Non-PRE generally applies to rentals/second homes.
Millage Property tax rate per $1,000 of taxable value. Millage varies by local unit (city/township), county, school district, and other authorities.
Taxable Value (TV) The value used to calculate property taxes in Michigan. TV is generally capped (Proposal A) and uncaps on transfer of ownership.
Assessed Value (AV) Typically 50% of a property’s True Cash Value (market value estimate) for assessment purposes.
PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance) Insurance often required when down payment is under 20%. Typically calculated annually and paid monthly.
Escrow Monthly collection for taxes and insurance held by the lender and paid when due.
Amortization A schedule showing how each payment is split between interest and principal over the life of the loan.
