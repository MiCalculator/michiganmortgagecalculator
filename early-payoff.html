Glossary Tap to expand

Amortization: A schedule showing how each payment splits into interest vs principal over time.

Extra payments: Additional principal reductions that can shorten payoff time and reduce total interest.

Lump-sum: A one-time extra principal payment applied immediately (month #1 here).

P&I: Principal and interest payment (does not include taxes/insurance/PMI/HOA).
